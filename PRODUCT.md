## Platform

web

## Users

- Students (school & college) in India seeking practical AI skills and career pathways.
- Working professionals upskilling into AI/GenAI roles.
- Parents & schools evaluating VibeKids (grades 3–12 AI-literacy platform).
- Prospective corporate/institutional partners of G-TEC Education.

## Product Purpose

FutureX AI Lab (an initiative of G-TEC Education) delivers AI education: structured certification programs (4 levels, 5 courses), hands-on labs, internships, industry projects, and placement support. Success = enrollment inquiries and course sign-ups.

## Positioning

Career-focused, practical AI education with a clear 4-level certification ladder (Generative AI → RAG pipelines → AI Agents & deployment → Foundation Models/FMOps + AWS practitioner readiness), backed by G-TEC Education's network. VibeKids: Socratic AI tutor ("Vibey") aligned to CBSE Circular Acad-15/2026, NEP 2020, NCF-SE 2023 — it guides reasoning instead of giving answers.

## Product Principles

1. Career outcomes first: every program maps to named job roles.
2. Practical over theoretical: labs, projects, deployment.
3. Accessible to rural and urban learners alike.
4. A clear ladder: 4 levels, each building on the last.
5. Never give answers a learner should reason to (VibeKids ethos).
